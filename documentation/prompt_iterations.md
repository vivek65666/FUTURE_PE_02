# Prompt Iteration & Refinement

## 1. Overview

The initial prompts were refined through multiple stages to
improve relevance, authenticity, consistency, and conversion
intent.

The iteration process focused on making the generated content
more useful for real short-form advertising.

---

## 2. Initial Prompt Approach

The initial approach focused on asking AI to generate UGC-style
advertising content for Salon Nayana.

The basic requirement was:

- Create UGC-style content
- Promote the salon
- Include a CTA

### Limitation

A simple instruction can produce content that is too generic,
too promotional, or not sufficiently adapted to the business.

---

## 3. Business Context Improvement

The prompts were improved by adding specific business context.

The context includes:

- Business name
- Business type
- Location
- Target audience
- Services

### Improvement

Adding business-specific information helps the AI generate content
that is more relevant to Salon Nayana instead of generic salon
advertising copy.

---

## 4. Structure Improvement

The UGC script prompt was structured around:

**Hook → Problem → Solution → Benefit → CTA**

### Improvement

This provides a consistent advertising flow.

The structure helps ensure that each script:

1. Gets attention
2. Identifies a customer consideration
3. Introduces a relevant service option
4. Explains a customer benefit
5. Provides a next step

---

## 5. Hook Variation

Instead of generating similar opening lines, the hook prompt
introduced different hook categories.

These include:

- Problem-based
- Curiosity
- Question
- Relatable situation
- Before-you-book
- Local
- Service-specific
- Awareness
- Personal preference
- Direct attention

### Improvement

Different hook angles allow the same business to test different
creative approaches.

---

## 6. Authenticity Improvement

The prompts were refined to prevent misleading UGC content.

The final prompts explicitly prohibit:

- Fake testimonials
- False personal experiences
- Invented customer stories
- Invented prices
- Invented discounts
- Fake urgency
- Unsupported claims
- Guaranteed results

### Improvement

The content can maintain a UGC-style feel without falsely
presenting the creator as a real customer.

---

## 7. Platform Adaptation

The content was adapted for:

- Instagram Reels
- Instagram Ads
- YouTube Shorts

### Improvement

Each platform receives different instructions for:

- Opening hook
- Pacing
- Visual direction
- Spoken content
- CTA

This prevents the same advertisement from being copied
unchanged across every platform.

---

## 8. CTA Improvement

The CTA system was expanded into multiple categories.

These include:

- Direct booking
- Service exploration
- Hair colour
- Hair treatments
- Curls & texture
- Local
- Soft CTA

### Improvement

Different CTAs can be selected based on the viewer's intent and
the purpose of the advertisement.

---

## 9. Output Formatting Improvement

The prompts were designed with predefined output structures.

For example, UGC scripts contain:

- Hook
- Problem
- Solution
- Benefit
- CTA
- Full spoken script

Platform-specific content contains:

- Hook
- Visual direction
- Spoken script
- On-screen text
- CTA

### Improvement

Structured outputs make the content easier to review, edit,
reuse, and deliver to a client or marketing team.

---

## 10. Quality Control

A quality-check section was added to the prompts.

The final content is checked for:

- Natural language
- Business relevance
- Clear conversion intent
- Platform suitability
- Authenticity
- Accuracy
- Appropriate CTA
- Absence of unsupported claims

---

## 11. Final Prompt Framework

The refined framework is:

**Role**
+
**Business Context**
+
**Target Audience**
+
**Objective**
+
**Content Framework**
+
**Platform Requirements**
+
**Tone**
+
**Authenticity Constraints**
+
**Output Format**
+
**Quality Check**

---

## 12. Result

The final prompt system produces a reusable UGC advertising
workflow for Salon Nayana.

It generates:

- Multiple hooks
- UGC ad scripts
- Platform-specific content
- Conversion-focused CTAs

The same framework can be adapted to other local businesses by
changing the business context, target audience, services, and
campaign objective.
