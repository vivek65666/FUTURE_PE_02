# CTA Quality Check

The CTAs were designed to:

- Be short and easy to speak
- Sound conversational
- Support conversion intent
- Avoid aggressive sales language
- Avoid fake urgency
- Avoid invented discounts or offers
- Avoid unsupported claims
- Match Salon Nayana's available services
- Work across short-form video platforms
