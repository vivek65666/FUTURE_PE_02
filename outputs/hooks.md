# Salon Nayana – UGC Ad Hooks

## Hook 1 – Problem

**Hook:**

Still not sure what hairstyle actually suits you?

**Angle:**

Addresses the common problem of choosing a hairstyle without
knowing what fits your preferences.

---

## Hook 2 – Curiosity

**Hook:**

What if your next hair look starts with the right consultation?

**Angle:**

Creates curiosity around choosing a service based on individual
hair needs.

---

## Hook 3 – Question

**Hook:**

Thinking about changing your hair but don't know where to start?

**Angle:**

Targets customers who want a new look but are unsure which
service to choose.

---

## Hook 4 – Relatable Situation

**Hook:**

You save a hundred hairstyle pictures, but still can't decide
which one is right for you.

**Angle:**

Uses a familiar situation to connect with people planning a
hair transformation.

---

## Hook 5 – Before You Book

**Hook:**

Before you book your next hair service, know what you're
actually looking for.

**Angle:**

Encourages viewers to think about their needs before selecting
a service.

---

## Hook 6 – Bengaluru

**Hook:**

If you're in Bengaluru and looking for a new hair or beauty
look, take a look at this.

**Angle:**

Uses local relevance to attract potential customers in
Bengaluru.

---

## Hook 7 – Service Specific

**Hook:**

Balayage, global colour, or highlights—which one fits your
look?

**Angle:**

Uses specific colour services to attract customers interested
in changing their hair colour.

---

## Hook 8 – Awareness

**Hook:**

Choosing a hair treatment just because it's trending may not
always be the right approach.

**Angle:**

Creates awareness around choosing treatments based on individual
hair needs.

---

## Hook 9 – Personal Preference

**Hook:**

Your ideal hair look should start with what works for you.

**Angle:**

Focuses on personalization rather than following a generic trend.

---

## Hook 10 – Direct Attention

**Hook:**

Looking for your next hair or beauty service in Bengaluru?
Keep watching.

**Angle:**

Directly identifies the target audience and creates a reason
to continue watching.

---

## Hook Quality Check

The hooks were designed to:

- Sound conversational
- Avoid traditional advertising language
- Create curiosity or relevance
- Target local Bengaluru customers
- Support different hair and beauty services
- Avoid fabricated testimonials
- Avoid unsupported guarantees or claims
