# Salon Nayana – Platform-Specific UGC Content

## Content Concept

**Campaign Theme:** Choosing the right hair and beauty service

**Business:** Salon Nayana

**Location:** Brigade Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka

**Target Audience:** Local Bengaluru customers interested in
haircuts, styling, colour, curls, texture, beauty services,
and hair treatments.

---

# 1. Instagram Reels

## Hook

Thinking about changing your hair but don't know where to start?

## Visual Direction

Creator speaks directly to camera while showing hairstyle
inspiration images or simple hair styling visuals.

## Spoken Script

"Thinking about changing your hair but don't know where to
start? There are so many options, from haircuts and colour
to curls, texture, and hair treatments. Salon Nayana in
Bengaluru offers different services you can explore based on
your preferences. Take a look at the services and find an
option that fits your next look."

## On-Screen Text

**Planning a new hair look?**

**Haircuts | Colour | Curls | Treatments**

## CTA

**Explore Salon Nayana's Services**

---

# 2. Instagram Ads

## Hook

Not sure which hair service is right for your next look?

## Problem

Choosing between a haircut, colour service, curls and texture
service, or hair treatment can feel confusing when you have
multiple options.

## Solution

Salon Nayana offers a range of hair and beauty services in
Bengaluru that customers can explore based on their individual
preferences.

## Benefit

Customers can compare available options and choose a service
that fits their preferred look and needs.

## Visual Direction

Start with the creator looking at different hairstyle
inspiration images.

Cut to simple visuals representing haircuts, colour, styling,
and treatment options.

End with a clear text overlay showing the CTA.

## Spoken Script

"Not sure which hair service is right for your next look?
Maybe you're thinking about a haircut, a new colour, curls,
or a hair treatment. Instead of choosing randomly, explore
your options first. Salon Nayana in Bengaluru offers a range
of hair and beauty services you can consider based on your
preferences. Explore the services and find what fits your
next look."

## CTA

**Explore Services**

---

# 3. YouTube Shorts

## Hook

Your next hair look doesn't have to start with a random
choice.

## Story Flow

1. Identify the problem of choosing between different services.
2. Introduce the range of services available.
3. Encourage viewers to explore options based on their needs.
4. End with a simple CTA.

## Visual Direction

- Opening: Creator facing camera.
- Middle: Show hairstyle inspiration or service-related visuals.
- Transition: Show text labels for haircuts, colour, curls,
  texture, and treatments.
- Ending: Display Salon Nayana and the CTA.

## Spoken Script

"Your next hair look doesn't have to start with a random
choice. If you're considering a haircut, colour, curls,
texture, or a hair treatment, start by exploring your
options. Salon Nayana in Bengaluru offers different hair
and beauty services to consider based on your preferences.
Take a look at the services and choose what fits your next
look."

## CTA

**Explore Salon Nayana's Services**

# Content Quality Check

The platform-specific versions were designed to:

- Maintain the same core campaign message
- Adapt the opening for each platform
- Use conversational UGC-style language
- Support short-form vertical video
- Include clear conversion intent
- Provide simple visual directions
- Avoid fabricated testimonials
- Avoid unsupported claims
- Avoid invented prices, discounts, and offers
- Avoid presenting the speaker as an actual customer
