## UGC Content Quality Check

The scripts were designed to:

- Use conversational language
- Start with attention-focused hooks
- Follow the Problem → Solution → Benefit → CTA framework
- Support short-form video content
- Maintain clear conversion intent
- Avoid fabricated customer testimonials
- Avoid claiming personal experience
- Avoid unsupported guarantees
- Avoid invented prices, discounts, or offers
- Use only the services included in the business context
