# UGC Hook Generation Prompt

## Purpose

Generate multiple short, attention-grabbing UGC-style hooks for
Salon Nayana's social media advertising content.

The hooks should feel natural, conversational, and relatable rather
than like traditional advertisements.

---

## Business Context

**Business Name:** Salon Nayana

**Business Type:** Hair & Beauty Salon

**Location:** Brigade Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka

**Services:**

- Haircuts
- Hair Colour
- Curls & Texture
- Beauty Services
- Keratin
- Straightening
- Hair Botox

---

## Prompt

Act as a UGC content strategist and short-form advertising
copywriter.

Create 10 different hooks for a UGC-style advertisement for
Salon Nayana.

The hooks should be suitable for short-form video content such
as Instagram Reels, Instagram Ads, and YouTube Shorts.

### Target Audience

Local customers in Bengaluru who are interested in hair,
beauty, styling, colour, curls, texture, and hair treatment
services.

### Hook Requirements

Each hook should:

- Capture attention within the first few seconds
- Sound conversational and natural
- Avoid sounding like a traditional advertisement
- Focus on a customer need, problem, curiosity, or desired outcome
- Be short enough to speak naturally in a video
- Be relevant to Salon Nayana
- Encourage the viewer to continue watching

### Hook Categories

Create hooks using different approaches:

1. Problem-based hook
2. Curiosity hook
3. Question hook
4. Relatable situation
5. Before-you-book hook
6. Local/Bengaluru hook
7. Service-specific hook
8. Mistake/awareness hook
9. Personal-preference hook
10. Direct attention hook

### Authenticity Rules

Do not:

- Invent customer experiences
- Create fake testimonials
- Claim personal use of the salon
- Make unsupported performance claims
- Guarantee results
- Use exaggerated claims such as "best salon in Bangalore"
- Invent prices, discounts, offers, or promotions

The hooks should be UGC-style, but they should not falsely
represent the speaker as an actual customer.

### Output Format

Return the result in this format:

## Hook 1 – Problem

**Hook:**
[Hook text]

**Angle:**
[Brief explanation]

## Hook 2 – Curiosity

**Hook:**
[Hook text]

**Angle:**
[Brief explanation]

Continue until all 10 hooks are provided.

### Final Quality Check

Before providing the final answer, verify that:

- All hooks are different
- The language sounds natural when spoken
- The hooks are relevant to Salon Nayana
- No unsupported claims are included
- The hooks are suitable for short-form video
- The hooks create a reason to keep watching
