# UGC Ad Script Generation Prompt

## Purpose

Generate short, authentic UGC-style advertising scripts for
Salon Nayana that are suitable for short-form video platforms.

The scripts should feel conversational and relatable rather than
like traditional polished advertisements.

---

## Business Context

**Business Name:** Salon Nayana

**Business Type:** Hair & Beauty Salon

**Location:** Brigade Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka

**Services:**

- Haircuts
- Hair Colour
- Curls & Texture
- Beauty Services
- Keratin
- Straightening
- Hair Botox

---

## Prompt

Act as a UGC advertising strategist and short-form video
copywriter.

Create 5 different UGC-style advertisement scripts for
Salon Nayana.

Each script should follow this basic framework:

**Hook → Problem → Solution → Benefit → CTA**

The scripts should be designed for approximately 20–30 seconds
of spoken video content.

---

## Target Audience

Target local customers in Bengaluru who are interested in:

- Haircuts
- Hair styling
- Hair colour
- Curls and texture
- Beauty services
- Hair treatments

The audience may be considering a new look, exploring service
options, or looking for a salon that fits their individual needs.

---

## UGC Style Requirements

The scripts should:

- Sound like natural spoken language
- Use short and easy-to-say sentences
- Feel conversational and relatable
- Avoid overly polished advertising language
- Focus on a real customer problem or consideration
- Present Salon Nayana as a relevant option
- Include a clear but natural CTA
- Be suitable for vertical short-form video

---

## Script Variety

Create 5 scripts using different angles:

### Script 1 – Haircut / New Look

Focus on someone considering a haircut or styling change.

### Script 2 – Hair Colour

Focus on someone considering balayage, global colour, or
highlights.

### Script 3 – Curls & Texture

Focus on customers looking for services suited to their natural
hair texture or curls.

### Script 4 – Hair Treatments

Focus on customers exploring Keratin, Straightening, or
Hair Botox.

### Script 5 – General Hair & Beauty

Create a broader script introducing Salon Nayana's hair and
beauty service options.

---

## Authenticity Rules

Do not:

- Invent personal customer experiences
- Write fake testimonials
- Claim that the speaker personally visited Salon Nayana
- Invent before-and-after results
- Guarantee a particular outcome
- Claim that Salon Nayana is the "best" salon
- Invent prices, discounts, offers, or promotions
- Make medical or treatment guarantees
- Add services that are not included in the business context

The content should be UGC-style advertising, but it must not
misrepresent the speaker as a real customer.

---

## CTA Requirements

Each script should end with a natural CTA such as:

- Book an appointment
- Explore the services
- Learn more
- Check the available options
- Visit the salon

Do not use aggressive sales language.

---

## Output Format

Return each script using this structure:

# Script 1 – [Angle]

### Hook

[Opening line]

### Problem

[Customer problem or consideration]

### Solution

[How Salon Nayana can be presented as an option]

### Benefit

[Relevant customer benefit without unsupported claims]

### CTA

[Natural call to action]

### Full Spoken Script

[Complete 20–30 second script]

---

Repeat the same structure for all 5 scripts.

---

## Final Quality Check

Before providing the final output, verify that:

- Every script has a different angle
- The opening hook is attention-grabbing
- The language sounds natural when spoken
- The scripts follow Hook → Problem → Solution → Benefit → CTA
- The content is suitable for short-form video
- The scripts have clear conversion intent
- No fake testimonials are included
- No unsupported claims are included
- No invented offers or prices are included
