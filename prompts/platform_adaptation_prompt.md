# Platform-Specific UGC Content Prompt

## Purpose

Adapt UGC-style advertising content for different short-form
content platforms while maintaining the same core business
message.

The goal is to create platform-specific content that feels
natural on each platform while maintaining clear conversion
intent.

---

## Business Context

### Business Name

Salon Nayana

### Business Type

Hair & Beauty Salon

### Location

Brigade Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka

### Target Audience

Local customers in Bengaluru interested in hair, beauty,
styling, colour, curls, texture, and hair treatment services.

### Services

- Haircuts
- Hair Colour
- Curls & Texture
- Beauty Services
- Keratin
- Straightening
- Hair Botox

---

## Prompt

Act as a short-form content strategist specializing in UGC
advertising.

Adapt a UGC-style advertising concept for the following
platforms:

1. Instagram Reels
2. Instagram Ads
3. YouTube Shorts

The core business message should remain consistent, but the
hook, pacing, structure, visual direction, and CTA should be
adapted to the requirements of each platform.

---

## Core UGC Framework

Use the following structure when appropriate:

**Hook → Problem → Solution → Benefit → CTA**

The content should feel like natural short-form social media
content rather than a traditional polished advertisement.

---

# Platform Requirements

## 1. Instagram Reels

Create a natural and engaging short-form video script.

### Requirements

- Strong opening hook
- Conversational language
- Quick pacing
- Short and easy-to-speak sentences
- Relatable customer situation
- Simple visual/action suggestions
- Natural CTA
- Approximately 20–30 seconds

### Style

The content should feel casual, relatable, and native to
Instagram Reels.

Avoid overly formal or corporate advertising language.

---

## 2. Instagram Ads

Create a more conversion-focused version of the UGC concept.

### Requirements

- Attention-grabbing opening
- Clearly identify the customer need
- Present Salon Nayana as a relevant option
- Communicate a relevant customer benefit
- Include a clear CTA
- Maintain a conversational UGC tone
- Avoid aggressive sales language
- Approximately 20–30 seconds

### Style

The content should balance authenticity with conversion intent.

---

## 3. YouTube Shorts

Create a concise, retention-focused version.

### Requirements

- Strong opening within the first few seconds
- Simple storytelling
- Short sentences
- Clear problem-to-solution progression
- Fast and easy-to-follow structure
- Natural CTA
- Approximately 20–30 seconds

### Style

The script should maintain attention throughout the short video
and avoid unnecessary information.

---

# UGC Style Guidelines

All platform versions should:

- Sound authentic and conversational
- Feel natural when spoken aloud
- Use simple and clear vocabulary
- Avoid polished corporate advertising language
- Focus on a customer need or consideration
- Maintain clear conversion intent
- Be suitable for vertical short-form video
- Keep the core business message consistent
- Adapt naturally to each platform

---

# Visual Direction

Where appropriate, include simple visual suggestions such as:

- Creator speaking directly to camera
- Showing hairstyle inspiration
- Showing hair styling preparation
- Showing relevant salon environment
- Showing hair colour inspiration
- Showing service-related visuals
- Text overlays highlighting the main point
- Ending with a CTA screen

Visual suggestions should not imply that a real customer
experience occurred unless that experience is verified.

---

# Accuracy and Authenticity Rules

Do not:

- Invent customer testimonials
- Claim the speaker personally visited Salon Nayana
- Claim personal use of a service without evidence
- Invent before-and-after results
- Guarantee a specific result
- Claim Salon Nayana is the "best salon"
- Invent prices
- Invent discounts
- Invent promotional offers
- Invent reviews or ratings
- Add services that are not included in the business context
- Make unsupported performance claims
- Make medical or treatment guarantees

The content should be UGC-style advertising without falsely
representing the speaker as an actual customer.

---

# CTA Guidelines

Use a natural CTA appropriate to the platform.

Possible CTAs include:

- Explore the services
- Explore hair colour options
- Explore hair treatments
- Book an appointment
- Learn more
- Check the available services
- Explore Salon Nayana

Avoid aggressive CTAs such as:

- Buy now
- Don't miss out
- Act immediately
- Limited time only

unless a real offer has been provided as input.
