# UGC Ad CTA Generation Prompt

## Purpose

Generate natural, conversion-focused calls-to-action for
UGC-style advertising content for Salon Nayana.

The CTAs should encourage viewers to take the next step without
sounding overly promotional or aggressive.

---

## Business Context

### Business Name

Salon Nayana

### Business Type

Hair & Beauty Salon

### Location

Brigade Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka

### Target Audience

Local customers in Bengaluru interested in haircuts, styling,
hair colour, curls, texture, beauty services, and hair treatments.

### Services

- Haircuts
- Hair Colour
- Curls & Texture
- Beauty Services
- Keratin
- Straightening
- Hair Botox

---

## Prompt

Act as a UGC advertising copywriter.

Generate 15 different CTAs for Salon Nayana's short-form
advertising content.

The CTAs should be suitable for:

- Instagram Reels
- Instagram Ads
- YouTube Shorts

Create a mixture of:

1. Direct CTAs
2. Soft CTAs
3. Exploration CTAs
4. Booking CTAs
5. Service-specific CTAs
6. Local CTAs

---

## CTA Requirements

Each CTA should:

- Be short and easy to say
- Sound conversational
- Have clear conversion intent
- Match a UGC-style advertisement
- Encourage an appropriate next step
- Be relevant to Salon Nayana
- Avoid aggressive sales language

---

## CTA Categories

### Direct Booking

Encourage the viewer to book or enquire about a service.

### Service Exploration

Encourage the viewer to explore specific services.

### Hair Colour

Encourage viewers interested in colour to explore colour
services.

### Hair Treatments

Encourage viewers to explore Keratin, Straightening, or Hair
Botox services.

### Curls & Texture

Encourage viewers to explore curls and texture services.

### Local

Encourage Bengaluru customers to explore Salon Nayana.

### Soft CTA

Use a low-pressure CTA that encourages viewers to learn more
before making a decision.

---

## Authenticity Rules

Do not:

- Invent discounts
- Invent limited-time offers
- Create false urgency
- Guarantee results
- Claim Salon Nayana is the best salon
- Invent customer testimonials
- Claim the speaker personally used the salon
- Invent prices
- Add unsupported services

The CTAs should create conversion intent without misleading
the audience.
